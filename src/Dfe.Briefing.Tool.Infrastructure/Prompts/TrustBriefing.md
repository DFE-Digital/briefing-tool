# Trust Name

## Trust overview

One paragraph: the trust's type and status, its TRN and the date it was established; its registered address and its website; how many academies it has, split by phase and by local authority, taken from the Academy counts; its total capacity and number of pupils on roll, as the trust record gives them; and any academies due to join. Name no people here - they go under Governance - and list no academies here - they go under Academies in the trust.

## Governance

- One bullet for each leader the trust record names - chief executive, accounting officer, chief financial officer, chair of trustees - with the role, the name and the appointment date, and the term end date where given. If one person holds two roles, give them one bullet naming both roles.
- Members: one nested bullet for each member, with how they were appointed and their start date.
- Trustees: one nested bullet for each trustee, with how they were appointed, their start date and their end date.

Name only people the trust's own record gives in its governance or leadership fields, and leave out a role the records don't give. If the records give none of these people, leave the whole section out, heading included.

## Academies in the trust

- Academy name: URN; type of school; phase; age range; local authority; capacity and number on roll; website.
- One bullet for every academy in the academies' records, each only once, in alphabetical order. Write "Not available" for a fact the records don't give for that academy. Give no Ofsted judgements, results, pupil figures or attendance here - each has its own section below.

## Ofsted summary

One sentence giving by_latest_ofsted_inspection from the Academy counts, in its order - then, if the Academy counts give report_card_academies_with_an_area_graded, one sentence giving it. Never count judgements or area grades across academies yourself.

Then one bullet for every academy, starting with the academy's name and a colon:

- Academy name: for a graded inspection, the overall effectiveness grade and the date of the latest inspection; for a report card, "Report card" and the date; for an inspection with neither, "Not judged" and the date; with no inspection in the records, "Not available".
  - One nested bullet for each other judgement from that same latest inspection (for example quality of education, or for a report card each area and its grade), then safeguarding and any category of concern, as the records give them.
  - Previous inspection: one nested bullet with the date of the inspection before the latest, and its overall effectiveness grade, or for a report card each area and its grade. Leave this out if there is none.

Put the academies in this order: graded inspections, most serious overall grade first (Inadequate, Requires improvement, Good, Outstanding); then report cards, those with an area graded Urgent improvement first, then Needs attention, then the rest; then Not judged; then Not available - alphabetical within each.

## KS2 summary

- Academy name: the percentage meeting the expected standard in reading, writing and maths, with the academic year, and the local authority and national figures where the records give them.
- One bullet for each academy with KS2 results, highest result first. If the trust has no primary academies, write "The trust has no academies with KS2 results." as the only line.

## KS4 summary

- Academy name: Attainment 8 score, with the academic year, and the local authority and national figures where the records give them.
  - One nested bullet for each other KS4 measure the records give for that academy: Progress 8, the EBacc average point score, the English and maths element scores, the percentages achieving grades 5 to 9 in English language, English literature and maths, and the percentage achieving grade 5 or above in English and maths - each with the local authority and national figures where the records give them.
- One bullet for each academy with KS4 results, highest Attainment 8 score first. If the trust has no secondary academies, write "The trust has no academies with KS4 results." as the only line.

## Socio-economic overview

- Whole trust: the trust-wide figures the trust record gives - for example pupils with an EHCP, on SEN support, eligible for free school meals, taking free school meals, and with English not as their first language - with the year they cover.
- Academy name: the same measures for that academy, as its records give them.
- One bullet for each academy, in alphabetical order. Leave out the Whole trust bullet if the records give no trust-wide figures, and leave out any measure the records don't give. If the records give none of these figures, leave the whole section out, heading included.

## Attendance

- Whole trust: overall absence and persistent absence, with the academic year, where the records give a trust-wide figure.
- Academy name: overall absence and persistent absence, with the academic year.
- One bullet for each academy with a figure, highest overall absence first. Include this section only if the records give at least one attendance or absence figure; otherwise leave it out completely, heading included.

Every academy is named exactly as the records give it, as plain text at the start of its own bullet - never in square brackets, and never left out.

Give no Rise or Recast concerns and no financial health, anywhere in the briefing. Never name anyone mentioned in a Rise or Recast concern, or give a pupil number, date of birth, address, phone number or email address from one - the only people named are those under Governance.
